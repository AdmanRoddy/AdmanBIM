01 OPEN A MODEL

Open a Navisworks .nwd or .nwc file from Files, iCloud Drive, OneDrive or a shared drive. The file is read right here on this device and nothing is uploaded.

Large models over 1 GB open in seconds, and detail fills in as you move around, just like Navisworks.

On a computer you can also drag a file onto this window.
